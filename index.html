SNAP Stocking & Shopping Checklist

2026 retailer standards • 28 qualifying variety targets • designed for a small convenience store

Important: This is a proposed inventory plan, not USDA certification. Verify the ingredients, varieties and perishable status of each specific SKU against the USDA rule and calculator. Have at least 3 saleable units of each variety continuously; the 3 perishable categories represented here are Dairy, Protein and Produce. Shelf-stable products still need expiration checks. Product photos are real listing photos from Walmart or other product-image sources; packaging can change. Images need an internet connection to load. Items marked “On old list” are not automatically confirmed in current stock.
Old inventory match: The blue “On old list” labels refer to products from your uploaded checklist, not confirmed quantities today. Inspect the shelf and enter today’s count. Your original jerky and ravioli are not automatically counted as qualifying varieties.
Product photographs show actual packaging found in web listings and may reflect another pack size. Walmart links open the item page or a product search; Google photos links show more images. Images load from online sites and need internet access. Actual retail availability, package sizes, ingredient lists and shelf life vary. The store should rotate inventory FEFO (first-expiring, first-out), inspect temperatures, and keep supplier receipts.
Sources: USDA Sample Pathways · USDA Variety Rules. Inventory stored locally in this browser only (not synced across devices).
