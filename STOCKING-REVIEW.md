# SNAP stocking plan review — October 28, 2026

The list has 28 planned varieties, seven in each category. Its category structure matches the November 4, 2026 inventory standard. This is a planning review, not an official store determination. Current quantities, saleability, exact package labels, and continuous display have not been inspected. The exact Great Value American Cheese Singles ingredient panel remains unverified.

Source: [USDA final stocking guidance](https://www.fna.usda.gov/snap/retailer/stocking-standards-rule). Maintain at least three normal sale units per variety throughout each operating day: 84 total, including a perishable variety in three categories. Unopened shelf-stable food does not become perishable merely because it requires refrigeration after opening.

USDA’s [sample pathways](https://www.fna.usda.gov/snap/retailer/stocking-standards-rule/pathways) explicitly illustrate bananas, canned olives, tomato paste, dried apricots, grape juice, soy milk, grated Parmesan, pancake mix, cornmeal and canned stew. These examples support product types; they do not approve every brand or formulation.

## Product evidence and unresolved checks

- [Silk original shelf-stable soy milk](https://www.silk.com/plant-based-products/shelf-stable/shelf-stable-original-soymilk/): manufacturer lists soymilk first and says unopened cartons can remain in the pantry.
- [Media Crema](https://www.goodnes.com/media-crema/products/media-crema-regular-76-oz-can/): light cream is first; the [manufacturer storage FAQ](https://www.goodnes.com/media-crema/faq/) says store unopened in a cool, dry place.
- [DINTY MOORE canned beef stew](https://www.hormel.com/brands/dinty-moore-beef-stew/product/beef-stew/): the manufacturer page shows the 15 oz can and lists beef stock followed by beef. The [9 oz microwave tray](https://www.hormelfoodservice.com/products/dinty-moorer-stew-beef-69oz-oz/) instead lists water, potatoes, then beef; it must not be assumed to fill the beef variety.
- [Great Value cheese SKU 10452423](https://www.walmart.com/ip/10452423): listing confirms a refrigerated sliced American cheese product, but its current ingredient panel was not readable in the source. Keep its classification provisional until the actual label or USDA review confirms it. A plain block cheese is an alternative in the same non-shredded variety; it would replace this row rather than add a variety.
- [100% Concord grape juice](https://www.walmart.com/ip/12927454291): listing identifies 100% grape juice. Do not substitute a grape-flavored drink.

Seven planned entries per category leave no spare variety if a SKU fails the label check or sells out. Keep a purchasing buffer and check stock daily. Brand, size, flavor, or fresh/canned form alone does not automatically create another variety. For bananas, use three individual fruit only when sold individually; otherwise count the normal sale bunch.
