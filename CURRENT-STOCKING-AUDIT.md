## What prevents a verified pass

1. **Cheese:** the selected entry is Great Value American Cheese Singles, but the cooler photo appears to show Kraft Singles. The exact Great Value ingredient statement was not readable in the current retailer source. Match the actual SKU and its label to a qualifying non-shredded cheese. This is not evidence that the product fails; it remains unverified. Plain block cheese or cheese sticks are explicit USDA examples in that slot if a substitution is needed.
2. **Nine products not confirmed in store:** evaporated milk, powdered milk, grated Parmesan, whole-wheat spaghetti, pancake mix, all-purpose flour, rice, cornmeal and fresh bananas. They are required slots in this particular 28-entry plan. “Not confirmed” does not establish that they are absent; check the shelves before buying.
3. **Depth of stock:** confirm three saleable units in every slot. Three cheese slices do not replace three retail cheese packages; three dry-milk inner pouches do not replace three retail boxes if sold boxed. Individually sold cereal/yogurt cups and juice bottles count individually; whole multipacks count when sold as packs. Count bananas as normally sold.
4. **Perishables:** the plan uses Dairy (milk, chocolate milk, yogurt), Protein (cold commercial sandwiches), and Produce (fresh bananas). Unopened shelf-stable juice, canned soup and Parmesan are not made perishable by displaying them in a cooler. Removing bananas without a qualifying alternative could lose the Produce perishable category.
5. **Continuous sale:** keep the qualifying stock normally displayed and available for sale throughout operation. Follow package storage requirements; inspect condition and remove unsaleable goods. The app's entered counts and dates do not independently verify this.
